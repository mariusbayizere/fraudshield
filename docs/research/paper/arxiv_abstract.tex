\begin{abstract}
We ask what FraudShield-EAC, a seeded synthetic benchmark of East African digital payments, can and cannot show. One velocity feature separates fraud at an
AUC of \nGateFloor{} alone, so the gate ranking metrics are reported as margins over it.

On all \nFvTrainRows{} training rows, an XGBoost-LightGBM ensemble reaches an AUC of
\nFvAUC{} (margin \nFvMargin{}). Its recall at the flag threshold, \nFvRecallSix{}, misses the
target of \nTargetRecall{}, and no training volume tried closed the gap. At a fixed
\nTrainUsed{} rows, the corpus they came from moved mean recall at the flag threshold up to
\nSelMeanRange{}, against \nFvSeedGainFixed{} from volume on one corpus (splits also larger); recall at a \nFPRBudget{} false-positive budget moved by
similar amounts under either change, and AUC more with volume. No interval was computed, seed noise
widens the range, and the analyses are pre-specified in
repository history but not blind. On the diagnostic model, leave-one-group-out ablations
measure redundancy, and leave-one-country-out and a novel variant held out in time are null.
Refitting the full-volume model without each fraud type in turn (never fitting on it, not never
seeing it) costs little recall at that
budget for velocity, SIM-swap and mule fraud and much more for agent
fraud and synthetic identity.

The one variant pre-specified in repository history, a single transfer rather than a multi-row
burst, is the one variant where detection was found to fail. It is modelled on a documented Kenyan scam but sent to a
listed payee, a choice the cited sources do not support, and was committed with the diagnostic
model's predicted recall before its first committed evidence. Neither production model flags it at
the flag threshold; at the budget the \nTrainUsed{}-row model (seed 1) catches
\nGateRevNewBudgetCaught{} of \nGateRevNewBudgetRows{} rows sent to a never-paid recipient and none of
\nGateRevKnownBudgetRows{} sent to a payee paid before, small counts from an observational split that isolate
no cause. No fraud parameter is sourced.
\end{abstract}
