\begin{abstract}
We ask what FraudShield-EAC, a seeded synthetic benchmark of East African digital payments (\nChannels{}
channels in \nCountries{} countries), can and cannot show. It is easy in a specific way: one velocity
feature separates fraud from legitimate payments at an AUC of \nGateFloor{} alone, so the production
model's ranking metrics are reported as margins over it.

Trained on all \nFvTrainRows{} training rows, an XGBoost and LightGBM ensemble reaches an AUC
of \nFvAUC{}, a margin of \nFvMargin{}. Its recall at the specified flag threshold, \nFvRecallSix{},
misses the target of \nTargetRecall{}, and more training data did not close the gap in any run
measured. At a fixed \nTrainUsed{} rows, the corpus they came from moved mean recall at the flag
threshold by up to \nSelMeanRange{}, against \nFvSeedGainFixed{} from volume on one corpus; recall at
the budget moved by similar amounts under either change, and AUC more with volume. No interval was computed for these
differences. On the diagnostic model, leave-one-group-out ablations measure redundancy, and
leave-one-country-out and a variant held out in time are null. Refitting without each fraud type in
turn costs little recall at a \nFPRBudget{} false-positive budget for velocity, SIM-swap and mule
fraud and much more for agent fraud and synthetic identity.

The one variant pre-specified in repository history, a single transfer rather than a multi-row
burst, is the one detection failure found. It is modelled on a documented Kenyan scam but sent to a
listed payee, a choice the cited sources do not support, and was committed with the diagnostic model's predicted
recall before its evidence. Neither production model flags it at the flag threshold; at the budget the
\nTrainUsed{}-row model catches \nGateRevNewBudgetCaught{} of \nGateRevNewBudgetRows{} rows sent to a
never-paid recipient and none of \nGateRevKnownBudgetRows{} sent to a known payee, small counts from
an observational split that do not isolate a cause. No fraud parameter is sourced; we state what a validation on real data would
need to show.
\end{abstract}
