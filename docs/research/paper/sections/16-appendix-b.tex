\section{Feature catalogue}
\label{app:features}

The \nFeatures{} features of the registry at \texttt{m4-complete}, by group, with the first sentence
of each definition (shortened where it carries parameters; the registry, \texttt{ml/src/\allowbreak fraudshield\_ml/\allowbreak features/\allowbreak registry.py},
holds the full definitions). The models train on \nTrainableFeatures{}: the six marked below are
either constant on this benchmark or have no source column in the generated data.

\begin{small}
\begin{longtable}{p{0.36\linewidth}p{0.56\linewidth}}
\toprule
Feature & Definition \\
\midrule
\endhead
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Velocity}} \\*
\texttt{velocity\_\allowbreak ratio\_\allowbreak 1h\_\allowbreak vs\_\allowbreak 30d} & Transactions in the trailing hour divided by the mean hourly count over the prior thirty days, both Laplace-smoothed so an account with no history scores one. \\
\texttt{tx\_\allowbreak count\_\allowbreak 60s} & Transactions on this account in the trailing sixty seconds before the scored transaction. \\
\texttt{tx\_\allowbreak count\_\allowbreak 1h} & Transactions on this account in the trailing hour before the scored transaction. \\
\texttt{tx\_\allowbreak count\_\allowbreak 24h} & Transactions on this account in the trailing day before the scored transaction. \\
\texttt{tx\_\allowbreak count\_\allowbreak 7d} & Transactions on this account in the trailing seven days before the scored transaction. \\
\texttt{amount\_\allowbreak sum\_\allowbreak 24h} & Sum of transaction amounts on this account in the trailing day, each converted to RWF at the FX rate for its own transaction date, not the scored transaction's date. \\
\texttt{amount\_\allowbreak sum\_\allowbreak 7d} & Sum of transaction amounts on this account in the trailing seven days, each converted to RWF at the FX rate for its own transaction date. \\
\texttt{unique\_\allowbreak counterparties\_\allowbreak 24h} & Distinct counterparty tokens this account transacted with in the trailing day. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Temporal}} \\*
\texttt{local\_\allowbreak hour\_\allowbreak sin} & Sine of the integer local hour on the daily circle, so late evening and midnight are adjacent. \\
\texttt{local\_\allowbreak hour\_\allowbreak cos} & Cosine partner of the local-hour sine, computed from the same integer hour. \\
\texttt{local\_\allowbreak day\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak week} & Local day of week, from local time rather than UTC. \\
\texttt{is\_\allowbreak local\_\allowbreak night} & True for local times from midnight to just before five in the morning. \\
\texttt{is\_\allowbreak month\_\allowbreak end\_\allowbreak window} & True on the last three and first two local days of a month, the salary period. \\
\texttt{seconds\_\allowbreak since\_\allowbreak last\_\allowbreak tx} & Seconds between this account's previous transaction and the scored one, with no window bound: the previous transaction counts however old it is. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Geographic}} \\*
\texttt{geo\_\allowbreak cell\_\allowbreak fraud\_\allowbreak rate\_\allowbreak 30d} & Confirmed-fraud proportion of the transaction's H3 resolution-6 cell over the prior thirty days, counting only labels whose label\_available\_at precedes the transaction timestamp, shrunk toward the training-fold base rate with a pseudo-count of 50. \\
\texttt{distance\_\allowbreak from\_\allowbreak last\_\allowbreak tx\_\allowbreak km} & Great-circle distance from this account's previous transaction location to the scored one, via the shared haversine primitive. \\
\texttt{implied\_\allowbreak speed\_\allowbreak kmh} & Distance from the previous transaction divided by the elapsed hours, capped above commercial cruising speed. \\
\texttt{distance\_\allowbreak from\_\allowbreak home\_\allowbreak centroid\_\allowbreak km} & Great-circle distance from the component-wise median of this account's prior ninety days of transaction locations to the scored one. \\
\texttt{is\_\allowbreak new\_\allowbreak country\_\allowbreak for\_\allowbreak account} & True when the account has never before sent to the counterparty's country, over unbounded history. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Amount behaviour}} \\*
\texttt{amount\_\allowbreak log1p} & log1p of the scored transaction's amount in RWF, converted at the FX rate for its own transaction date. \\
\texttt{amount\_\allowbreak zscore\_\allowbreak 90d} & Robust z-score of the amount against the account's prior ninety days, using the median and the scaled median absolute deviation. \\
\texttt{amount\_\allowbreak to\_\allowbreak max\_\allowbreak 90d\_\allowbreak ratio} & Scored amount divided by the largest amount on this account in the prior ninety days, both in RWF at their own transaction dates. \\
\texttt{round\_\allowbreak sum\_\allowbreak flag} & True when the amount is an exact multiple of one of the transaction currency's common denominations, read from the country pack rather than hard-coded (ADR 0023). \\
\texttt{just\_\allowbreak below\_\allowbreak limit\_\allowbreak flag} & True when the amount falls just below a channel or KYC-tier limit in force at the transaction's timestamp (ADR 0026): the structuring signal. \emph{(not trained: constant)} \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Counterparty}} \\*
\texttt{counterparty\_\allowbreak is\_\allowbreak new\_\allowbreak for\_\allowbreak account} & True when this account has never transacted with this counterparty before, over unbounded history. \\
\texttt{counterparty\_\allowbreak account\_\allowbreak age\_\allowbreak days} & Days between the counterparty account's opening date and the scored transaction. \emph{(not trained: no source)} \\
\texttt{counterparty\_\allowbreak unique\_\allowbreak senders\_\allowbreak 24h} & Distinct accounts that sent to this counterparty in the trailing day---the mule signal, since a legitimate recipient rarely acquires many unrelated senders at once. \\
\texttt{counterparty\_\allowbreak confirmed\_\allowbreak fraud\_\allowbreak 90d} & Confirmed-fraud transactions involving this counterparty in the prior ninety days, counting only labels whose label\_available\_at precedes the scored transaction. \\
\texttt{tx\_\allowbreak count\_\allowbreak to\_\allowbreak counterparty\_\allowbreak 30d} & Transactions from this account to this counterparty in the trailing thirty days---an established-relationship signal, low for a first transfer to a new payee. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Device and channel}} \\*
\texttt{channel} & The transaction's channel, passed to native categorical handling rather than one-hot encoded. \\
\texttt{device\_\allowbreak is\_\allowbreak new\_\allowbreak for\_\allowbreak account} & True when this account has not used this device fingerprint before. \\
\texttt{accounts\_\allowbreak per\_\allowbreak device\_\allowbreak 7d} & Distinct accounts that transacted from this device fingerprint in the trailing seven days---the device-sharing signal behind synthetic-identity rings. \\
\texttt{device\_\allowbreak changes\_\allowbreak 24h} & Distinct device fingerprints the account used in the trailing day, minus one. \\
\texttt{device\_\allowbreak age\_\allowbreak days} & Days since this device fingerprint was first seen anywhere in the institution, not merely on this account: a device first seen an hour ago is the signal, and scoping it to the account would make every device new on its first use there. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Account profile}} \\*
\texttt{account\_\allowbreak age\_\allowbreak days} & Days between the account's opening date and the scored transaction. \emph{(not trained: no source)} \\
\texttt{kyc\_\allowbreak tier} & The account's KYC tier in force at the transaction's timestamp (ADR 0026), as an ordinal. \emph{(not trained: no source)} \\
\texttt{days\_\allowbreak since\_\allowbreak sim\_\allowbreak swap} & Days since the most recent SIM swap on the account's registered MSISDN, from the MNO signal, counting only swaps before the scored transaction. \\
\texttt{dormancy\_\allowbreak reactivation\_\allowbreak flag} & True when this account had no transaction in the sixty days before the scored one, having had at least one before that---reactivation after dormancy, not a new account. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Agent}} \\*
\texttt{agent\_\allowbreak float\_\allowbreak utilisation\_\allowbreak ratio} & The agent's float drawn down as a fraction of its limit, as they stood at the transaction's timestamp (ADR 0026). \emph{(not trained: no source)} \\
\texttt{agent\_\allowbreak cashout\_\allowbreak count\_\allowbreak 1h} & Cash-out transactions at this agent in the trailing hour. \\
\texttt{agent\_\allowbreak unique\_\allowbreak customers\_\allowbreak 1h} & Distinct accounts transacting at this agent in the trailing hour. \\
\texttt{agent\_\allowbreak distance\_\allowbreak from\_\allowbreak registered\_\allowbreak km} & Great-circle distance from the agent's registered premises to the transaction location, via the shared haversine primitive---an agent transacting far from its registered site. \emph{(not trained: no source)} \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Corridor}} \\*
\texttt{corridor\_\allowbreak class} & Domestic, intra-bloc, cross-bloc within Africa, or intercontinental, from the two countries' bloc memberships (ADR 0023; Section~\ref{sec:setting}). \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{Synthetic identity}} \\*
\texttt{synthetic\_\allowbreak identity\_\allowbreak score} & A deterministic, hand-specified composite of low KYC tier, young account age, shared device and phone attributes, and rapid volume ramp, computed without labels. \\
\addlinespace
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\end{small}
