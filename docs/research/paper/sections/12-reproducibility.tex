\section{Reproducibility statement}
\label{sec:repro}

\paragraph{Data.} The benchmark is generated, not distributed as a fixed file: seed \nSeed{} and
the generator code at a stated commit are meant to reproduce a draw byte for byte, independently of
how the work is batched. A test checks byte-identity across batch sizes on small runs. The evaluation
draw itself was regenerated from the generator at \texttt{m4-complete} on 2026-10-19, on the
development laptop, and its fingerprint matched \texttt{d8083dbc}
(\texttt{evidence/\allowbreak regeneration\_\allowbreak d8083dbc.txt}); because the fingerprint
covers a fixed sample of each table's rows and the row counts, that is evidence of reproduction, not
a comparison of every row. Each draw is identified by a SHA-256 fingerprint over a fixed sample of
its rows and its row counts (\texttt{6abde44e} and \texttt{d8083dbc} in this paper), and its realism report records the parameter hash, the check
set hash and the fingerprint. The dataset release is licensed CC BY 4.0
(\texttt{dataset/release/}); the code is Apache-2.0.

\paragraph{Evidence.} Every number in this paper's text is mapped by \texttt{numbers.tex} to its source
(Appendix~\ref{app:evidence}) and checked against it at a named commit by
\texttt{audit\_numbers.py}. Most model results come from raw output files under
\texttt{docs/benchmarks/} whose header records the command, its exit status, the commit and whether
the working tree was clean. The exceptions are the single-feature measurement, whose file carries no
such stamp; the dataset figures, which come from the realism report, the parameter provenance and the
claims register; the figures quoted from the lab notebook and from ADRs; and the files produced for
this paper under \texttt{docs/\allowbreak research/\allowbreak paper/\allowbreak evidence/}, among
them the second round of evidence, whose headers record the commit and the command and, for most
files, the machine. Scoring runs on the test period are logged
in \texttt{docs/benchmarks/test\_set\_access.jsonl}, with earlier runs back-filled; the first of this paper's own reads (the reversal-label count,
\texttt{evidence/\allowbreak reversal\_\allowbreak labels\_\allowbreak d8083dbc.txt}) was back-filled after the fact, and the paper's evidence scripts now log their
own reads. The number
audit, the generated appendices, the figures and the compilation run in continuous integration on
every push (\texttt{.github/\allowbreak workflows/\allowbreak paper.yml}). Experiment
tracking in MLflow was not used for the evidence in this paper (ADR 0030); the evidence files carry the
provenance instead.

\paragraph{Hardware.} The evidence comes from runners and from the development laptop. PB-66's full-volume run, its
leave-one-fraud-type-out run and this paper's second round of evidence, including the frontier's
re-run, ran on GitHub-hosted runners, virtual machines on shared hardware. Most of these files' headers
record the runner's processor and memory; the frontier and single-feature files record no machine,
and that they ran on a runner is inferred from the paths in their recorded commands and from the
run's record (\texttt{evidence/\allowbreak round2\_\allowbreak run\_\allowbreak d8083dbc.txt}). The earlier evidence files whose stamps record a processor and memory
record an Intel Core \nHwCPU{} with \nHwCores{} logical cores and \nHwMem{}, the development laptop
(inferred from the processor; no file names the machine); other earlier files record fewer details or none, and that they were made on the same laptop is inferred.
Latency figures from either kind of machine are reported as shapes, not as gate numbers (ADR 0010).

\paragraph{One command.} A single target that regenerates the dataset, trains, evaluates and
produces the paper's tables and figures from a clean machine, with compute time and hardware
recorded, is not yet built; this milestone (M11) owns it. Until it exists, each figure is reproduced by the command recorded in its
evidence file.

\paragraph{Publication.} Scripts and checklists for publishing the dataset and model, for the author
to run, are part of this milestone and do not exist yet. No DOI exists, and none is cited.
