\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

FraudShield-EAC was built to develop and evaluate a fraud detection system for East African digital
payments without real customer data. Measured against it, the specified ensemble reaches an AUC of \nGateAUC{}, but the benchmark sets
a high floor: one velocity feature alone separates the test rows at
\nGateFloor{}, and the ensemble's margin over it on the same rows is \nGateMargin{}. Its recall at the flag
threshold misses the specified target, and \nLcVolumeFactor{} times the training data does not close
the gap for this model on this draw. Most of what
a reader would like to learn from the benchmark it cannot teach. Feature ablations measure redundancy,
because \nGroupsAboveCeiling{} disjoint groups each exceed the single-feature ceiling alone;
leave-one-country-out and a novel variant held out in time are null, which we infer follows from
fraud parameters not keyed by country and from the variant's multi-row shape respectively; and the one variant pre-specified without that structure is caught at
\nRevRecall{}, for reasons the experiment does not isolate. What survives is a methodology, the record
of how each of these limits was found, and a measurement on one laptop and one benchmark that the
cost of an exact explanation grows with the model while accuracy does not; that this cost does not
depend on how easy the data is, we infer and have not tested.

\paragraph{Future work.} The following work would turn these findings into claims about real payments.
First, validation on real, labelled transactions, under a data protection impact assessment and in a
permitted location, measuring the same floor, margins and calibration. Second, a held-out variant with
a new recipient, as we read the documented form of the reversal scam, scored with per-row model
outputs kept, which would separate the contributions of burst structure and counterparty novelty;
this paper's variant removes novelty from only some of its rows, and its per-row scores were not
kept. Next, the
variation of the headline figures across selections of training rows, which the learning curve
exposed at seed 1 and did not measure: several corpus depths and windows at a fixed volume, with the history
each training row has reported beside each result (PB-76). Last, the serving and load
measurements on dedicated hardware: the frontier at the \nPctlTail{}th percentile, end-to-end decision latency,
and scale to \nSpecRows{} rows: \notyet{M10}.
