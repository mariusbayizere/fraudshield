# NNNN — Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Requirements affected:** FR-xx-xx, NFR-xxx-xx, …
- **Defects referenced:** D-xx, …

## Context

What forces are at play: the requirement text, the defect, measured facts, constraints.
State facts with their source (SRS section, command output, upstream documentation).

## Options considered

1. **Option A** — description; consequences for safety, SRS fidelity, verifiability.
2. **Option B** — …

## Decision

The option chosen and the rule it follows (build prompt A.3 rule 1: safest for customers'
money and personal data, then most faithful to the SRS acceptance criterion, then simplest
to verify automatically).

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder, what must be monitored, and how the decision is verified
(test names, traceability rows).
